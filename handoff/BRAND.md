# Making Small Memories logo system

The new symbol begins with one small, central moment. Two open orbits show that moment continuing outward as a memory, while the offset spark suggests discovery and a life still unfolding. The open geometry keeps the mark warm and human rather than institutional, and it complements the line-art language already present throughout the five concepts.

The primary lockup keeps the company name as the dominant identifier. Its typography and geometry remain consistent across the collection. The symbol and period use each concept's accent color while the lettering and orbit lines inherit the surrounding foreground color, allowing one identity to work across every palette without separate artwork.

## Production assets

- `public/brand/making-small-memories-logo.svg`: primary horizontal lockup in the core warm palette.
- `public/brand/making-small-memories-mark.svg`: standalone symbol for compact applications.
- `public/favicon.svg`: app and browser icon derived from the symbol.
- `public/brand/social-lockup.svg`: social-card lockup paired with approved brand language.
- `public/images/social.jpg`: branded 1200 × 630 social-sharing image.
- `src/components/Brand.astro`: theme-aware web lockup used by the gallery, five concepts, privacy page, 404 page, and shared footers.

Keep clear space around the lockup at least equal to the diameter of its center dot. Do not stretch, rotate, outline, or rearrange the elements. On colored backgrounds, use the live component or recolor both dark-brown strokes consistently while retaining one contrasting accent.
