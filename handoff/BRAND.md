# Making Small Memories logo system

The new symbol begins with one small, central moment. Two open orbits show that moment continuing outward as a memory, while the offset spark suggests discovery and a life still unfolding. The open geometry keeps the mark warm and human rather than institutional, and it complements the selected website’s line-art language.

The primary lockup keeps the company name as the dominant identifier. Its typography and geometry remain consistent throughout the website. The symbol and period use the selected warm accent color while the lettering and orbit lines inherit the surrounding foreground color.

## Production assets

- `public/brand/making-small-memories-logo.svg`: primary horizontal lockup in the core warm palette.
- `public/brand/making-small-memories-mark.svg`: standalone symbol for compact applications.
- `public/favicon.svg`: app and browser icon derived from the symbol.
- `public/brand/social-lockup.svg`: social-card lockup paired with approved brand language.
- `public/images/social.jpg`: branded 1200 × 630 social-sharing image.
- `src/components/Brand.astro`: web lockup used by the homepage, privacy page, 404 page, and shared footer.

Keep clear space around the lockup at least equal to the diameter of its center dot. Do not stretch, rotate, outline, or rearrange the elements. On colored backgrounds, use the live component or recolor both dark-brown strokes consistently while retaining one contrasting accent.
